Overview

RASteer removes unwanted concepts from diffusion models while preserving the concepts users want to keep. It works at inference time without training or modifying model weights.

Method

RASteer builds a subspace from retained concepts. Retain Orthogonal Steering removes components aligned with this subspace from the erasure direction. Overlap Adaptive Calibration adjusts this removal at each layer and denoising step to balance erasure and preservation.

Results

612 to 13

Exposed body part detections on the I2P benchmark fall from 612 in the original model to 13 with RASteer.

Across unsafe content, instance, and artistic style erasure, RASteer matches or outperforms the evaluated activation steering and weight editing baselines while better preserving unrelated content.
